Washington, DC’s 4th Annual Sketch Comedy Festival

Instructions

Apply now to perform at DC Sketchfest! Please note: DC Sketchfest is dedicated to sketch comedy only—improv and stand-up acts will not be considered, and application fees are non-refundable. After submitting your form, you’ll be prompted to pay the DC Sketchfest application fee. Applications are incomplete without payment.

Important: All sales are final. Payment does not guarantee acceptance into the festival.

Deadlines

Early Application ($25) – October 20 to November 1.

Standard Application ($30) – November 1 to November 29.

Late Application ($40) – November 30 to December 13.

Applications close December 13, 11:59 PM ET.

Location

All shows are in Washington, DC. Venue to be announced.

Questions

Read our FAQs or email admin@dcsketchfest.com.

Notification: All applicants will be contacted mid-January 2027.

Submission guidelines for your tape
  • Tape length: submit a 15–20-minute video. Shorter submissions, such as a 5- or 10-minute tape, won’t provide enough material for our judges to score accurately. Material beyond 20 minutes will not be watched.
  • Content order: include your best material first and prioritize LIVE performance footage. Digital shorts (filmed content) are discouraged unless they strongly reflect your live show style.
  • Video format: to avoid playback and permission issues, we strongly recommend submitting a YouTube or Vimeo link instead of using file-sharing sites like Google Drive or Dropbox. If you have multiple videos, please edit them into a single video before submitting.
  • Judging process: your submission will be evaluated by a randomly assigned panel in the first round. Tailor your material for an audience unfamiliar with your previous work.

Email admin@dcsketchfest.com if you have additional questions.
