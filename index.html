What is DC Sketchfest?
DC Sketchfest is DC's only sketch comedy festival and features dozens of comedy troupes from the US and Canada for four days each spring. DC Sketchfest is organized by the DC sketch comedy community and founded by Bad Medicine and Rails Comedy.
When is the 2027 festival?
Dates will be announced this fall. Join the mailing list and we'll email you as soon as they're set.
What is sketch comedy?
A sketch is a short, fully scripted comedy scene. Sketch comedy is the same thing you see on Saturday Night Live!
Where do I get tickets?
Tickets aren't on sale yet. Once the 2027 dates are announced this fall, tickets for every show go on sale right here.
Can my team perform?
Performer applications open in October. Join the mailing list and we'll send the application link as soon as it's live, or email admin@dcsketchfest.com with questions.
Who’s that crazy bottle guy?
Oh, that’s Mumbie. The official DC Sketchfest mascot.
