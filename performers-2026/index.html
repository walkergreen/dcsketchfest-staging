ALoT (A Lot Of Things)

Chicago, IL

Piloted by Beau Maguire and Leo LaCamera, A Lot of Things (A.L.O.T.) delivers hilarious sketch comedy in unexpected ways. Will it be a choose your own adventure style revue? An eight person show performed by only two people? Beau and Leo's premier improv form, "Beau and Leo do 50 things in 20 minutes"? (No. It won't be that one, because this is a sketch fest - but it could be one of those other things!) Regardless of what form it takes, If you like big dumb comedy performed in new dumb ways, A.L.O.T. is the show for you!

Avery & Sabrina Are Doing A Sketch Show!

Dallas, TX

Avery and Sabrina have been performing sketch comedy for over four years. As founding members of the Dallas Comedy Club Sketch Team in 2023, they quickly became known for their on stage chemistry, talent, and dedication. Following numerous successful performances, Avery and Sabrina now serve as both coaches and performers, leading the DCC Sketch Team. Their latest venture, “Avery and Sabrina are Doing a Sketch Show,” premiered at the Dallas Independent Comedy Festival in April.
The show has since toured through Dallas Comedy Club, Out of Bounds Comedy Festival, New York Sketchiest, and Denver Sketchfest where the show won Best Of Fest - Ensemble.  Between Sabrina’s extensive improv background and Avery’s theater degree, these co-writers and friends(?) continue to develop their distinct brand of chaos and laughter on stages across these grand United States of America.

Bad Medicine

Washington, DC

The Bad Medicine Show is a high-energy sketch comedy spectacle that’s equal parts dark, cerebral, and delightfully absurd. It’s the perfect blend of smart and stupid fun from a merry band of DC friends who dive headfirst into the ridiculous. When we're not cracking up audiences at the legendary DC Improv Comedy Club, we’re hitting the road, bringing the laughs to comedy festivals like Toronto Sketchfest, SF Sketchfest, and Philly Sketchfest. Oh, and we don’t just perform, we teach the fine art of sketch comedy too.

Burnt Toast Sketch Show

Brooklyn, NY

Burnt Toast is a Brooklyn-based sketch group that formed out of a comedy group at the University of Oklahoma. We do fast-paced sketch comedy like you've never seen! The best part of our performances? We pack each show tight! If one sketch isn't your favorite, don't worry! We're just about to move on to the next one! Each member is a writer and performer, and collaborates on nearly every sketch written. Our members hail from all over, and came together at the hub of Gen-Z comedy: Brooklyn. In addition to sketch comedy, each member is an artist in other capacities: film, stage, and beyond! You can catch us semi-regularly around New York City!

Cam Wyllie

Somewhere in Canada

Cam Wyllie is Canada's beloved Sketch Comedy Maniac, with 3 Canadian Comedy Award Nominations, Festival Appearances all over North America, and a bunch of Comedy Awards with made up sounding names.He's coming in hot with a carefully curated selection of brave, tragic, and gleefully dumb characters who, like him, are just trying to do their best. Armed with a passable singing voice, a can do attitude, and the odd tinfoil prop, he'll give you plenty of reasons to laugh, cheer, and get invested. Open your heart to the hilarious, often surreal, and always unpredictable world of Cam Wyllie. Winner of the Toronto Sketchfest's Best of the Fest award, Denver Sketchfest's Best of the Fest, and one of only 2 acts to have won the $10,000 grand prize at the Cam Wyllie Sketch Comedy Invitational.

Catastrophic Success

Chicago, IL

Formed in the hallowed halls of The Second City Chicago in 2023, Catastrophic Sucess is a professional sketch group comprised of performer-writers Jake Jones, Mikey Fried, Grant Batdorff, and Sophia Brazda. Known for their surreal, absurdist style of sketch and heightened theatricality, this band of classically trained actors-turned-comedians bring life, music, and thought-provoking commentary to their work. (We're kidding.) Following a sold-out run at the Den Theatre of their self-produced revue "Catastrophe!" (2024), their follow-up show, "All's Well That Ends" (2025) would sell out the renowned Judy's Beat Lounge stage at the Second City Chicago and place Mainstage members Laurel Krabacher and Ryan Miera at the directing helm. At its core, Catastrophic Success finds its stride in exploring the cacophany of mundanity, calamity, and the art of failing upwards. In these sketches, failure is exalted and success...utterly catastrophic.

Chaps

Washington, DC

Chaps is DC’s first all-women sketch team, made of a suspender-clad, super group of highly seasoned jokesters from across the DC comedy scene. In keeping with their dapper aesthetic, Chaps' comedic stylings take inspiration from classic vaudeville acts — infusing their shows with jaunty music, physical gags, and buffoonish wit, a la Charlie Chaplin or Abbott and Costello. As a collective, they seek to create roles for each other that are not bound by feminine archetypes  / comedy tropes, instead  writing characters that are unapologetically silly, dark, surprising, absurd. Collectively, Chaps’ members have performed in more than a dozen  sketch festivals including Montreal Sketchfest, Sketchfest Seattle, Chicago's IO Fest, Philly Sketchfest, and NYC Sketchfest.

Danielle Steger

Washington, DC

Danielle Steger is a DC-based sketch comedian who has performed at Philly Sketchfest, SketchFest Seattle, and DC Sketchfest. She has also appeared at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe and Capital Fringe Festival, where she won Best of Fest in 2019 and 2023 with two different comedy shows. She's transgender, which you may already know because she brings it up CONSTANTLY.

DC Raclette Club

Washington, DC

First and foremost, DC Raclette Club is cheesy. Like the savory Swiss cheese from which they are named, they are rich, warm and best enjoyed with laughter. Inspired by acts like Mitchell & Webb, Aunty Donna, and Kids in the Hall, the trio of Andy, Isabelle and Sara bring big characters that celebrate queer identity and joy. Are they all friends? Lovers? Maybe two of them are married and the third is a best friend? Watch this Washington, DC-based group and see if you can figure it out.

For The Time Being

Washington, DC

For the Time Being is one of one. Like a perfectly ripe avocado in a world of hard, unyielding mangoes, their ability to make the innocuous into innuendo and the regular into ridiculous has been delighting audiences since their founding. Featuring writers and performers from the worlds of sketch, improv, animation, stand-up, and even birdwatching, their diverse backgrounds meld into energetic, varied, off-the-wall sketches. Forevermore

Baltimore, MD

Forevermore is Baltimore’s all women sketch comedy troupe that is working to fight fire with satire! As Charm City's only sketch group, Forevermore has been entertaining audiences from coast to coast since 2022 with sketches that are silly, stupid, sexual, and sometimes darkly comedic. The troupe consists of local comedians, actors, and dancers who have combined their unique talents to create parody sketches, original songs, elaborate dance numbers, and visual gags all with the intention of pushing the envelope, pressing your buttons, and poking your funny bone in all the right places.

Shitty Mozart

Brooklyn, NY

They cloned Mozart, it went horribly wrong and now we're stuck with the consequences. Wearing a cheap wig, Shitty Mozart (Aaron Nemo, a writer for The Late Show with Stephen Colbert) performs a dazzlingly chaotic and vulgar one-man multimedia concerto.Terrified that the audience will realize he lacks the musical genius of his predecessor, Shitty Mozart shows off his own talents – playing makeshift instruments he's invented, performing duets with cartoons he drew, and activating his robot hype man – in an effort to exact revenge on those who have wronged him.Shitty was a selection at SF Sketchfest, The New York Comedy Festival, Flyover Comedy Festival, Austin Sketchfest, Projectorfest in New York City, and Edinburgh Fringe. Come see what Beyond the Joke calls “the symphony of silliness you’ve been dreaming about” https://www.beyondthejoke.co.uk/content/15164/review-shitty-mozart

The Incredible Shrinking Matt & Jacquie

Philadelphia, PA

The Incredible Shrinking Matt & Jacquie are a Philadelphia based sketch comedy duo consisting of Matt Schmid and Jacquie Baker. The pair met while performing in the Philly Improv Theater house team The Flat Earth. They quickly bonded over a shared goofy yet dark sense of humor and decided to spin off their own side project as a duo.
